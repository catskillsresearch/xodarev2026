This development restates the eleven compared theorems (Theorems A, B, D, the refutations of Theorem 4.2(ii), Theorem B, Theorem C and the Theorem D residual hypothesis, and the two NFLVR definition-sharpness refutations) using only definitions built directly from the Brownian driver: the signature `paperSig2 B 0 t`, the signature filtration `sigFiltration`, the volatility `sigVol`, `kitItoInt` and `kitQuadVar`, the price predicate `IsSignaturePrice`, and the market `Market` with price `sigPrice`. The predicate `IsSignaturePrice` requires almost surely continuous paths on `[0, T]` and almost sure equality to the Doléans exponential at each time. The NFLVR conjunct of `theorem_B_mp_unwrapped` is Delbaen–Schachermayer NFLVR for the uniformly bounded-below admissible-gains cone of that process. Each `X_unwrapped` is proved from the library theorem `X`. For Theorem C, completeness uses gains in the closed span of elementary Brownian integrals (`elemGains`), proved from the MathFin completeness result by density of simple integrands.
