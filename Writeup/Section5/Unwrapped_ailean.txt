This module restates the nine compared theorems (Theorems A, B, D and the refutations of Theorem 4.2(ii), Theorem B, Theorem C and the Theorem D residual hypothesis) using only definitions built directly from the Brownian driver: the signature `paperSig2 B 0 t`, the signature filtration `sigFiltration`, the volatility `sigVol`, `kitItoInt` and `kitQuadVar`, the price predicate `IsSignaturePrice`, and the market `Market` with price `sigPrice`. Each `X_unwrapped` is proved from the library theorem `X`. For Theorem C, completeness uses gains in the closed span of elementary Brownian integrals (`elemGains`), proved from the MathFin completeness result by density of simple integrands. These are the declarations listed in `comparator.json`. The Lean source is `Unwrapped.lean`.
