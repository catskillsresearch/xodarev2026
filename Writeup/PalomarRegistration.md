## Official Palomar description

The following paragraph is the `project.description` field of `formalization.yaml`, reproduced verbatim. If Palomar takes its abstract from that field, this is the text it would print, under the project title "Xodarev Signature Volatility Models in Lean 4".

> A Lean 4 / Mathlib formalization of Akmal Xodarev, "On the Structural Foundations of Signature Volatility Models", arXiv:2605.17142. Palomar registration: TBD. Framing: prove the paper claims that hold, at the exact strength of the Lean theorems below, and refute the claims that do not, by Lean negations of those claims and nothing stronger. Proved: theorem_A — for every SignatureVolatilityModel (which includes d ≥ 1, T > 0, s₀ > 0, HypH1, HypH2, and parameter nondegeneracy ℓ ≠ 0) and every Brownian driver, under the additional analytic hypothesis HypH3 on the signature volatility, there exists a SignaturePriceSolution of the signature SDE (strictly positive, with Doléans–Dade representation, satisfying the integral equation, with square-integrable supremum, and a martingale on the prolonged-signature filtration), and this solution is unique among processes that are measurable with respect to the signature filtration, lie in KitStrongSolutionClass, and satisfy the integral equation, almost everywhere on [0, T]; theorem_B_mp — under the same HypH3, there exists a SignaturePriceSolution that is a martingale on the signature filtration, SatisfiesNFLVR holds for AdmissibleGainsUnderP, every signature coordinate is a special semimartingale, and the → direction of Theorem 4.2(ii) holds (if the exponential-affine conditional-expectation formula holds through some classical Riccati solution, then a classical Riccati solution exists). This is not a proof of the paper's full Theorem B, whose conjunction includes the ← direction of the iff; affine_riccati_duality_mp — for every model and driver and every finitely supported u₀ with finite signature transform, if the exponential-affine conditional-expectation formula holds through some classical Riccati solution, then a classical Riccati solution exists; theorem_D — for every MarketData and every X in L²(ℱ^S_T), there is a unique GKW decomposition X = (∫ X) · 1 + G + ε with G in the gains space H0 and orthogonal to constants, ε in L²(ℱ^S_T) ∩ H0⊥ and orthogonal to the gains set, and the Pythagorean identity. This does not identify G as a stochastic integral ∫ H dS. Refuted by Lean counterexamples: not_affine_riccati_duality_mpr — for every SignatureVolatilityModel and every Brownian driver, with no analytic HypH3 hypothesis, it is not the case that every finitely supported u₀ with finite signature transform and some classical Riccati solution admits a classical Riccati solution ψ for which E[exp⟨u₀, Ŵ_T⟩ | ℱ_t] = exp⟨ψ(T − t), Ŵ_t⟩ almost surely for all t ∈ [0, T]. The proof uses the family u₀^(λ) = 2λ e_[1,1,1] − λ e_[1,0] − 12λ² e_[1,1,1,1,0] (λ ≥ 1), for which ℛ(u₀^(λ)) = 0 and the signature transform is finite (Lean proves ∫ exp⟨u₀^(λ), Ŵ_t⟩ dP ≤ 1). Lean does not state a theorem that this exponential is a local martingale or that it is not a true martingale; what is compared is the failure of the universal conditional-expectation formula; not_affine_riccati_duality — under the same hypotheses (every model and driver, no HypH3), the paper's iff in Theorem 4.2(ii) is false as a universal statement over such u₀; not_theorem_B_mpr and not_theorem_B — the same ← failure, and the failure of the paper's full Theorem B conjunction, each stated with an additional unused HypH3 hypothesis, so they apply to every model and driver for which analytic HypH3 holds (HypH3 is satisfiable; it holds for constant volatility); not_theorem_C and theorem_C_refuted — for the Black–Scholes market (T > 0, s₀ > 0, σ₀ ≠ 0, one-dimensional Brownian motion) and every finite set of call strikes, the equivalence "complete with finitely many calls on ℱ^S_T ⇔ N^*_S < ∞" is false, because blackScholes_isCompleteWithCalls holds and the completeness depth is infinite (theorem_C_blackScholes_depth_infinite, blackScholes_completenessDepth_eq_top). Separately, theorem_C_depth_infinite states that N^*_S = ∞ and no finite-depth signature span is dense, for every MarketData whose L²(ℱ^S_T) is infinite-dimensional; not_prop58_blackScholes_depth_zero — Black–Scholes completeness depth is not 0; blackScholes_completenessDepth_eq_top — it equals ∞; not_lemma77_shuffle_estimate — for every weight w and every letter ℓ in Fin d, there is no constant C such that ‖e_I ⧢ e_J‖_w ≤ C ‖e_I‖_w ‖e_J‖_w for all words I, J; not_lemma77_of_hypH2 is the same conclusion with an unused HypH2 hypothesis, hence also for every H2 weight; not_lemma78_local_lipschitz — only for the weight w ≡ 1 (Weight.constOne), on ExtTensorAlgebra (d+2) for every d, the Riccati operator is not locally Lipschitz on bounded subsets of T_w; theorem_D_residual_hypothesis_fails — for Black–Scholes (same parameters as above), ¬ (N^*_S < ∞). This shows that the residual-expansion hypothesis of Theorem D fails for Black–Scholes; it does not refute the expansion identity on markets where the hypothesis holds. No open problems remain. Why each counterexample works: Writeup/narratives/ (one short file per refutation; verbatim outside source in Writeup/narratives/appendix/). Every compared theorem uses only propext, Quot.sound and Classical.choice. Project rule: no Lean `axiom` declarations. Akmal Xodarev did not participate in or endorse this formalization. Lean was written with AI-agent assistance under Lars Warren Ericson's direction and review.

## Claim-to-Lean strength table

Each row records exactly what the named Lean theorem proves, and nothing more.

| Paper claim | Lean theorem | Exact strength |
|---|---|---|
| Theorem A | `theorem_A` | Under the model's H1, H2 and analytic HypH3: a `SignaturePriceSolution` exists (strictly positive, Doléans representation, SDE, $L^2$-supremum, martingale on the signature filtration) and is unique among signature-adapted `KitStrongSolutionClass` solutions of the integral equation. |
| Theorem B (fragment) | `theorem_B_mp` | Under HypH3: martingale, NFLVR, special semimartingale coordinates, and $→$ Riccati. Not the paper's full Theorem B. |
| Thm 4.2(ii) $→$ | `affine_riccati_duality_mp` | If the affine formula holds through some classical Riccati solution, then a classical solution exists. No HypH3. |
| Thm 4.2(ii) $←$ | `not_affine_riccati_duality_mpr` | For every model and driver, no HypH3: the universal affine conditional-expectation formula fails. The proof uses $u_0^{(\lambda)}$ with $\mathcal{R}=0$ and $\int\exp\le 1$. Lean does not prove "not a true martingale." |
| Thm 4.2(ii) iff | `not_affine_riccati_duality` | Same hypotheses, no HypH3: the iff is false as a universal statement. |
| Theorem B $←$ / full B | `not_theorem_B_mpr`, `not_theorem_B` | Same $←$ failure and failure of the full conjunction, each with unused HypH3. |
| Theorem C iff | `not_theorem_C`, `theorem_C_refuted` | False for Black–Scholes ($T>0$, $s_0>0$, $\sigma_0\neq 0$, 1-d BM) and every finite strike set, because completeness holds and $N^*_S=\infty$. |
| BS completeness | `blackScholes_isCompleteWithCalls` | Complete on $\mathcal{F}^S_T$ with finitely many calls, for every finite strike set (the proof uses no calls). |
| Depth in general / BS | `theorem_C_depth_infinite`, `theorem_C_blackScholes_depth_infinite`, `blackScholes_completenessDepth_eq_top` | $N^*_S=\infty$ whenever $L^2(\mathcal{F}^S_T)$ is infinite-dimensional, and in particular for Black–Scholes. |
| Proposition 58 | `not_prop58_blackScholes_depth_zero` | Black–Scholes depth is not 0 (same BS parameters). |
| Theorem D, GKW part | `theorem_D` | Unique GKW projection in $L^2(\mathcal{F}^S_T)$. Does not identify $G$ as $\int H\,dS$. |
| Theorem D residual hypothesis | `theorem_D_residual_hypothesis_fails` | $\lnot(N^*_S<\infty)$ for Black–Scholes only; the expansion identity is not refuted. |
| Lemma 77 | `not_lemma77_shuffle_estimate`, `not_lemma77_of_hypH2` | No shuffle constant, for every weight (and, with unused HypH2, every H2 weight), already on basis words. |
| Lemma 78 | `not_lemma78_local_lipschitz` | Only for $w\equiv 1$, on `ExtTensorAlgebra (d+2)`: $\mathcal{R}$ is not locally Lipschitz on bounded sets of $T_w`. |
