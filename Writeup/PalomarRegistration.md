# Main results

Palomar registration: **TBD**.

The compared theorems are the eleven unwrapped headline results: Theorems A--D and their refutations, including the two NFLVR definition-sharpness refutations. They are proved from the corresponding library theorems, using only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. Gemini Carbon supplied the scaled-tensor disproof of the converse affine/Riccati duality and the geometric-grid doubling construction that separates literal and terminal-only NFLVR from Delbaen--Schachermayer NFLVR.

## Official Palomar description

The following paragraph is the official project description, reproduced verbatim, under the title "Xodarev Signature Volatility Models in Lean 4".

> A Lean 4 / Mathlib formalization of Akmal Xodarev, "On the Structural Foundations of Signature Volatility Models", arXiv:2605.17142. Palomar registration: TBD. Framing: prove the paper claims that hold, at the exact strength of the Lean theorems below, and refute the claims that do not, by Lean negations of those claims and nothing stronger. Theorems A–D and their refutations are stated in unwrapped form, using only definitions built directly from the Brownian driver B: the signature Ŵ_t = paperSig2 B 0 t of (t, W_t), the signature filtration sigFiltration B generated by its coordinates, the volatility sigVol M B = ⟨ℓ, Ŵ_t⟩, the Itô integral kitItoInt B and the quadratic variation kitQuadVar B. Each X_unwrapped is proved from the library theorem X (theorem_A, …), which states the corresponding claim through the ProlongedBrownianSetup / ItoCalculusKit packaging; the unwrapped definitions agree with the packaged ones definitionally. The packaged SignaturePriceSolution represents the price by the Doléans exponential at every outcome; the unwrapped predicate IsSignaturePrice requires almost surely continuous paths on [0, T] and almost sure equality to that exponential at each time. Compared: theorem_A_unwrapped, theorem_B_mp_unwrapped, theorem_D_unwrapped (proved); not_affine_riccati_duality_mpr_unwrapped, not_affine_riccati_duality_unwrapped, not_theorem_B_mpr_unwrapped, not_theorem_B_unwrapped, not_theorem_C_unwrapped, theorem_D_residual_hypothesis_fails_unwrapped, not_literal_nflvr_unwrapped, not_terminal_bdd_nflvr_unwrapped (refutations). Proved: theorem_A_unwrapped — for every SignatureVolatilityModel (which includes d ≥ 1, T > 0, s₀ > 0, HypH1, HypH2, and parameter nondegeneracy ℓ ≠ 0) and every Brownian driver, under the additional analytic hypothesis HypH3 on the signature volatility, there exists a price process S with IsSignaturePrice (S₀ = s₀, measurable, adapted to the signature filtration, strictly positive, with almost surely continuous paths on [0, T] and, at each t ∈ [0, T], almost sure equality to the Doléans exponential s₀ exp(∫σ dW − ½∫σ² ds), satisfying the integral equation S_t = s₀ + ∫_0^t S σ dW almost surely for each t ∈ [0, T], with square-integrable supremum, and a martingale on the signature filtration), and S is unique among processes that are measurable with respect to the signature filtration, lie in KitStrongSolutionClass, and satisfy the integral equation, almost everywhere on [0, T]; theorem_B_mp_unwrapped — under the same HypH3, there exists such an S that is a martingale on the signature filtration, SatisfiesNFLVR_DS holds for the Delbaen–Schachermayer cone BoundedBelowGains of uniformly bounded-below admissible strategies whose integrand H S σ lies in the ℱ^W-predictable L² space and whose wealth is a continuous genuine L² Itô integral against W⁰ (narrower than the paper's local class ∫ H² d⟨S⟩ < ∞ almost surely), for that same process S, every signature coordinate is a special semimartingale for the signature filtration, and the → direction of Theorem 4.2(ii) holds (if the exponential-affine conditional-expectation formula holds through some classical Riccati solution, then a classical Riccati solution exists). This is not a proof of the paper's full Theorem B, whose conjunction includes the ← direction of the iff; theorem_D_unwrapped — for every Market (a model, a Brownian driver and HypH3, with price S = sigPrice, the Doléans exponential) and every X in L²(ℱ^S_T), there is a unique GKW decomposition X = (∫ X) · 1 + G + ε with G in the gains space H0 and orthogonal to constants, ε in L²(ℱ^S_T) ∩ H0⊥ and orthogonal to the gains set, and the Pythagorean identity. This does not identify G as a stochastic integral ∫ H dS. Refuted by Lean counterexamples: not_affine_riccati_duality_mpr_unwrapped — for every SignatureVolatilityModel and every Brownian driver, with no analytic HypH3 hypothesis, it is not the case that every finitely supported u₀ with finite signature transform and some classical Riccati solution admits a classical Riccati solution ψ for which E[exp⟨u₀, Ŵ_T⟩ | ℱ_t] = exp⟨ψ(T − t), Ŵ_t⟩ almost surely for all t ∈ [0, T]. The proof uses the family u₀^(λ) = 2λ e_[1,1,1] − λ e_[1,0] − 12λ² e_[1,1,1,1,0] (λ ≥ 1), for which ℛ(u₀^(λ)) = 0 and the signature transform is finite (Lean proves ∫ exp⟨u₀^(λ), Ŵ_t⟩ dP ≤ 1). Lean does not state a theorem that this exponential is a local martingale or that it is not a true martingale; what is compared is the failure of the universal conditional-expectation formula; not_affine_riccati_duality_unwrapped — under the same hypotheses (every model and driver, no HypH3), the paper's iff in Theorem 4.2(ii) is false as a universal statement over such u₀; not_theorem_B_mpr_unwrapped and not_theorem_B_unwrapped — the same ← failure, and the failure of the paper's full Theorem B conjunction, each stated with an additional unused HypH3 hypothesis, so they apply to every model and driver for which analytic HypH3 holds (HypH3 is satisfiable; it holds for constant volatility); not_theorem_C_unwrapped — for the Black–Scholes market (T > 0, s₀ > 0, σ₀ ≠ 0, one-dimensional Brownian motion) and every finite set of call strikes, the unconditional equivalence "complete with finitely many calls on ℱ^S_T ⇔ N^*_S < ∞" is false, and the paper's separate claim that Black–Scholes has completeness depth N^*_S = 0 is likewise false: the market is complete, but under Definition 55 one has N^*_S = ∞. This is not a counterexample inside Theorem 56's finite-polynomial subcase K(N^*_S) < ∞ with a strike set rich enough to span polynomials in S_T of degree at most K(N^*_S). Here complete means that every X in L²(ℱ^S_T) is almost surely c + Σ_k a_k (S_T − k)^+ + G with G in the L²-closed span of the elementary Brownian integrals Σ_p h_p (W_{p.2} − W_{p.1}) over intervals 0 ≤ p.1 ≤ p.2 ≤ T with h_p bounded and σ(W_u : u ≤ p.1)-measurable; theorem_D_residual_hypothesis_fails_unwrapped — for Black–Scholes (same parameters as above), ¬ (N^*_S < ∞). This shows that the residual-expansion hypothesis of Theorem D fails for Black–Scholes; it does not refute the expansion identity on markets where the hypothesis holds; not_literal_nflvr_unwrapped — for Black–Scholes (T > 0, s₀ > 0, σ₀ ≠ 0, 1-dimensional Brownian motion), literal NFLVR (Defs 2.17 and 4.3, no wealth lower bound) fails: a geometric-grid doubling strategy, transferred by H = K/(S σ₀), is S-admissible and ends at V_T = 1 a.s.; not_terminal_bdd_nflvr_unwrapped — the same free lunch is in the terminal- only bounded-below cone of Section 4.6. Neither claim refutes Delbaen–Schachermayer NFLVR, which requires a uniform-in-time lower bound and, in the Lean statement, an integrand H S σ in ℱ^W-predictable L² with wealth a continuous genuine L² Itô integral against W⁰, narrower than the paper's local class, and is proved at that strength for the unwrapped signature-price process in theorem_B_mp_unwrapped. The geometric-grid doubling construction and the distinction among the three NFLVR wordings, as well as the scaled-tensor disproof of the converse Riccati duality, are due to Gemini Carbon. Supporting results, proved in the library with no sorry but not compared: the wrapped forms theorem_A, theorem_B_mp, theorem_D, not_affine_riccati_duality_mpr, not_affine_riccati_duality, not_theorem_B_mpr, not_theorem_B, not_theorem_C (with MathFin's Itô integral of square-integrable predictable integrands as the gains), theorem_D_residual_hypothesis_fails, not_literal_nflvr and not_terminal_bdd_nflvr; affine_riccati_duality_mp — for every model and driver and every finitely supported u₀ with finite signature transform, if the exponential-affine conditional-expectation formula holds through some classical Riccati solution, then a classical Riccati solution exists; theorem_C_refuted — the same refutation of the unconditional equivalence and of the Black–Scholes depth claim as not_theorem_C; blackScholes_isCompleteWithCalls — Black–Scholes is complete on ℱ^S_T with finitely many calls, for every finite strike set; theorem_C_blackScholes_depth_infinite and blackScholes_completenessDepth_eq_top — its completeness depth is infinite; theorem_C_depth_infinite — N^*_S = ∞ and no finite-depth signature span is dense, for every MarketData whose L²(ℱ^S_T) is infinite-dimensional; not_prop58_blackScholes_depth_zero — Black–Scholes completeness depth is not 0; not_lemma77_shuffle_estimate — for every weight w and every letter ℓ in Fin d, there is no constant C such that ‖e_I ⧢ e_J‖_w ≤ C ‖e_I‖_w ‖e_J‖_w for all words I, J; not_lemma77_of_hypH2 is the same conclusion with an unused HypH2 hypothesis, hence also for every H2 weight; not_lemma78_local_lipschitz — only for the weight w ≡ 1 (Weight.constOne), on ExtTensorAlgebra (d+2) for every d, the Riccati operator is not locally Lipschitz on bounded subsets of T_w. Every compared claim is either proved or refuted at the stated strength. A short mathematical narrative is given for each counterexample. Every compared theorem uses only propext, Quot.sound and Classical.choice. Project rule: no Lean `axiom` declarations. Akmal Xodarev did not participate in or endorse this formalization. Lean was written with AI-agent assistance under Lars Warren Ericson's direction and review.

## Claim-to-Lean strength table

Each row records exactly what the named Lean theorem proves, and nothing more. Rows marked *compared* name the unwrapped theorem, which states the claim of the corresponding library theorem directly in terms of the Brownian driver and is proved from it; the other rows are proved in the library and not compared.

| Paper claim | Lean theorem | Exact strength |
|---|---|---|
| Theorem A | `theorem_A_unwrapped` (*compared*; library `theorem_A`) | Under the model's H1, H2 and analytic HypH3: a price process `S` with `IsSignaturePrice` exists (strictly positive, almost surely continuous paths on $[0,T]$, almost sure Doléans representation at each time in $[0,T]$, SDE, $L^2$-supremum, martingale on the signature filtration) and is unique among signature-adapted `KitStrongSolutionClass` solutions of the integral equation. The packaged `SignaturePriceSolution` used by `theorem_A` remains a pointwise Doléans representation. |
| Theorem B (fragment) | `theorem_B_mp_unwrapped` (*compared*; library `theorem_B_mp`) | Under HypH3: martingale, Delbaen--Schachermayer NFLVR for uniformly bounded-below admissible strategies whose integrand $HS\sigma$ lies in $\mathcal{F}^W$-predictable $L^2$ and whose wealth is a continuous genuine $L^2$ Itô integral against $W^0$ (narrower than the paper's local class), special semimartingale coordinates, and $\to$ Riccati. In the compared form the NFLVR statement is for the signature-price process $S$ itself. The bundled `theorem_B_mp` asserts the same NFLVR for a continuous version of the packaged solution, which equals that solution almost surely at each time. Not the paper's full Theorem B. |
| Thm 4.2(ii) $\to$ | `affine_riccati_duality_mp` | If the affine formula holds through some classical Riccati solution, then a classical solution exists. No HypH3. |
| Thm 4.2(ii) $\leftarrow$ | `not_affine_riccati_duality_mpr_unwrapped` (*compared*; library `not_affine_riccati_duality_mpr`) | For every model and driver, no HypH3: the universal affine conditional-expectation formula fails. The proof uses $u_0^{(\lambda)}$ with $\mathcal{R}=0$ and $\int\exp\le 1$. Lean does not prove "not a true martingale." |
| Thm 4.2(ii) iff | `not_affine_riccati_duality_unwrapped` (*compared*; library `not_affine_riccati_duality`) | Same hypotheses, no HypH3: the iff is false as a universal statement. |
| Theorem B $\leftarrow$ / full B | `not_theorem_B_mpr_unwrapped`, `not_theorem_B_unwrapped` (*compared*; library `not_theorem_B_mpr`, `not_theorem_B`) | Same $\leftarrow$ failure and failure of the full conjunction, each with unused HypH3. |
| Unconditional completeness iff | `not_theorem_C_unwrapped` (*compared*); library `not_theorem_C`, `theorem_C_refuted` | The unconditional equivalence "complete with finitely many calls $\Leftrightarrow N^*_S<\infty$", for an arbitrary finite strike set, is false for Black--Scholes ($T>0$, $s_0>0$, $\sigma_0\neq 0$, 1-d BM): completeness holds and, under Definition 55, $N^*_S=\infty$. This also refutes the paper's separate Black--Scholes depth claim ($N^*_S=0$). It is not a counterexample inside Theorem 56's finite-polynomial subcase $K(N^*_S)<\infty$ with a sufficiently rich strike set. In the compared form, complete means every $X\in L^2(\mathcal{F}^S_T)$ is a.s. $c+\sum_k a_k(S_T-k)^+ + G$ with $G$ in the $L^2$-closed span of elementary Brownian integrals $\sum_p h_p(W_{p_2}-W_{p_1})$ on $[0,T]$; the library form uses MathFin's Itô integral. |
| BS completeness | `blackScholes_isCompleteWithCalls`, `bsMarket_isCompleteWithCallsUnwrapped` | Complete on $\mathcal{F}^S_T$ with finitely many calls, for every finite strike set (the proof uses no calls). |
| Depth in general / BS | `theorem_C_depth_infinite`, `theorem_C_blackScholes_depth_infinite`, `blackScholes_completenessDepth_eq_top` | $N^*_S=\infty$ whenever $L^2(\mathcal{F}^S_T)$ is infinite-dimensional, and in particular for Black--Scholes. |
| Proposition 58 | `not_prop58_blackScholes_depth_zero` | Black--Scholes depth is not 0 (same BS parameters). |
| Theorem D, GKW part | `theorem_D_unwrapped` (*compared*; library `theorem_D`) | Unique GKW projection in $L^2(\mathcal{F}^S_T)$. Does not identify $G$ as $\int H\,dS$. |
| Theorem D residual hypothesis | `theorem_D_residual_hypothesis_fails_unwrapped` (*compared*; library `theorem_D_residual_hypothesis_fails`) | $\lnot(N^*_S<\infty)$ for Black--Scholes only; the expansion identity is not refuted. |
| Literal NFLVR (Defs 2.17+4.3) | `not_literal_nflvr_unwrapped` (*compared*; library `not_literal_nflvr`) | Free lunch $V_T=1$ a.s. in the Black--Scholes market; no uniform-in-time bound is claimed. |
| Terminal-only bounded-below NFLVR (Sec. 4.6) | `not_terminal_bdd_nflvr_unwrapped` (*compared*; library `not_terminal_bdd_nflvr`) | The same free lunch, since $V_T\ge 0$ a.s. Does not refute Delbaen--Schachermayer NFLVR. |
| Lemma 77 | `not_lemma77_shuffle_estimate`, `not_lemma77_of_hypH2` | No shuffle constant, for every weight (and, with unused HypH2, every H2 weight), already on basis words. |
| Lemma 78 | `not_lemma78_local_lipschitz` | Only for $w\equiv 1$, on `ExtTensorAlgebra (d+2)`: $\mathcal{R}$ is not locally Lipschitz on bounded sets of $T_w`. |
