**Definition** (`ExtTensorAlgebra.concat`). Given $a, b$ in `ExtTensorAlgebra d`, their concatenation product `concat a b` (denoted with left-associative infix notation `a ⊗ₜ b` at precedence $70$) is the element of `ExtTensorAlgebra d` whose coefficient at each word $I$ (`I : Word d`) is
$$\sum_{k \in \mathrm{range}(I.\mathrm{length} + 1)} a.\mathrm{coeff}(I.\mathrm{take}\, k)\, b.\mathrm{coeff}(I.\mathrm{drop}\, k).$$

**Lemma** (`ExtTensorAlgebra.concat.of_basisWord`). For any words $I, J$ in `Word d`, the concatenation of canonical basis elements satisfies `basisWord I ⊗ₜ basisWord J = basisWord (I ++ J)`.

Proof. By extensionality on `ExtTensorAlgebra d`, fix a word $L$ and compare coefficients after unfolding `concat` and `basisWord`, splitting into cases on whether $L = I \mathbin{+\!+} J$. When $L = I \mathbin{+\!+} J$, the index $I.\mathrm{length}$ belongs to `Finset.range ((I ++ J).length + 1)` (`List.length_append` and linear arithmetic), so `Finset.sum_eq_single` collapses the sum to $k = I.\mathrm{length}$: at $k = I.\mathrm{length}$, `List.take_left` and `List.drop_left` give $(I \mathbin{+\!+} J).\mathrm{take}\, I.\mathrm{length} = I$ and $(I \mathbin{+\!+} J).\mathrm{drop}\, I.\mathrm{length} = J$, making the summand $1$; for any other $k \le (I \mathbin{+\!+} J).\mathrm{length}$ with $k \ne I.\mathrm{length}$, `List.length_take_of_le` implies $((I \mathbin{+\!+} J).\mathrm{take}\, k).\mathrm{length} = k \ne I.\mathrm{length}$, so $(I \mathbin{+\!+} J).\mathrm{take}\, k \ne I$ and the summand vanishes. When $L \ne I \mathbin{+\!+} J$, the right-hand side is $0$, and `Finset.sum_eq_zero` shows every summand on the left vanishes: if $L.\mathrm{take}\, k = I$, then $L.\mathrm{drop}\, k \ne J$ since otherwise `List.take_append_drop` would reconstruct $L = (L.\mathrm{take}\, k) \mathbin{+\!+} (L.\mathrm{drop}\, k) = I \mathbin{+\!+} J$, contradicting $L \ne I \mathbin{+\!+} J$; and if $L.\mathrm{take}\, k \ne I$, the first factor is already $0$.
