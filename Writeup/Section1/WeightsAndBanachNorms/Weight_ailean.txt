**Structure** (`Weight`). A *weight* is a sequence $w : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ (with a weight coerced directly to its underlying function on $\mathbb{N}$) that has these properties:

- It is normalized at level zero: $w(0) = 1$.
- It is strictly positive at every level: $w(n) > 0$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$.
- It is monotone nondecreasing on $\mathbb{N}$: whenever $m \le n$, $w(m) \le w(n)$.

**Lemma** (`Weight.one_le`). For any weight $w$ (`w : Weight`) and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $1 \le w(n)$.

Proof. Rewriting $1$ as $w(0)$ via `w.w_zero`, the inequality follows from monotonicity `w.mono` applied to $0 \le n$ (`Nat.zero_le n`).

**Lemma** (`Weight.inv_le_one`). For any weight $w$ (`w : Weight`) and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $(w(n))^{-1} \le 1$.

Proof. This is a direct application of `inv_le_one_of_one_le₀` to `w.one_le n`.
