**Lemma** (`memL2wInv_of_memTw`). For any weight $w$ (`w : Weight`) and $a$ in `ExtTensorAlgebra d`, if `ha : MemTw w a` holds, then `MemL2wInv w a` holds and $\mathrm{norm2wInv}(w, a) \le \mathrm{normW}(w, a)$ (`norm2wInv w a ≤ normW w a`).

Proof. Every term $w(n)^2\, \mathrm{levelNormSq}(a, n)$ is nonnegative (`mul_nonneg` applied to `sq_nonneg` and `ExtTensorAlgebra.levelNormSq.nonneg a n`). For each $N \in \mathbb{N}$, rewriting $w(n)^2\, \mathrm{levelNormSq}(a, n) = (w(n)\, \mathrm{levelNorm}(a, n))^2$ via `mul_pow` and `ExtTensorAlgebra.levelNorm.sq` (`Finset.sum_congr`), the finite $\ell^2$-to-$\ell^1$ inequality `sum_sq_le_sq_sum` on `Finset.range N` (using $0 \le w(n)\, \mathrm{levelNorm}(a, n)$ from `(w.pos n).le` and `ExtTensorAlgebra.levelNorm.nonneg`) bounds $\sum_{n \in \mathrm{range}(N)} (w(n)\, \mathrm{levelNorm}(a, n))^2$ by $\bigl(\sum_{n \in \mathrm{range}(N)} w(n)\, \mathrm{levelNorm}(a, n)\bigr)^2$. Since `ha` (`MemTw w a`) gives $\sum_{n \in \mathrm{range}(N)} w(n)\, \mathrm{levelNorm}(a, n) \le \mathrm{normW}(w, a)$ via `Summable.sum_le_tsum`, squaring both sides (`pow_le_pow_left₀`, using nonnegativity of the partial sum via `Finset.sum_nonneg`) yields $\sum_{n \in \mathrm{range}(N)} w(n)^2\, \mathrm{levelNormSq}(a, n) \le \mathrm{normW}(w, a)^2$ uniformly in $N$. Consequently, `summable_of_sum_range_le` establishes summability `hsum : MemL2wInv w a`, and `Summable.tsum_le_of_sum_range_le` bounds the full series $\sum'_n w(n)^2\, \mathrm{levelNormSq}(a, n) \le \mathrm{normW}(w, a)^2$. Taking square roots via `Real.sqrt_le_sqrt` and simplifying $\sqrt{\mathrm{normW}(w, a)^2} = \mathrm{normW}(w, a)$ with `Real.sqrt_sq` and `normW.nonneg w a` yields `norm2wInv w a ≤ normW w a`.
