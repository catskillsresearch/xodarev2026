**Theorem** (`weighted_truncation_estimates`). Given a weight `w : Weight` and elements `ℓ, a : ExtTensorAlgebra d` with `MemL2w w ℓ` and `MemL2wInv w a`, we have `|dualPairing ℓ a| ≤ norm2w w ℓ * norm2wInv w a`, and for every truncation level `N : ℕ` the tail `projGt N ℓ` satisfies `MemL2w w (projGt N ℓ)` together with `|dualPairing (projGt N ℓ) a| ≤ norm2w w (projGt N ℓ) * norm2wInv w a`.

Proof. First prove a general weighted Cauchy–Schwarz pairing bound for any `L : ExtTensorAlgebra d` satisfying `MemL2w w L`. For each `n : ℕ`, since `w n ≠ 0` by `w.pos`, the levelwise inner-product bound `|L.levelInner a n| ≤ L.levelNorm n * a.levelNorm n` (`ExtTensorAlgebra.levelInner.abs_le`) rewrites as `|L.levelInner a n| ≤ (L.levelNorm n / w n) * (w n * a.levelNorm n)`. Summing over `n ∈ Finset.range M` for an arbitrary `M : ℕ` and applying the finite Cauchy–Schwarz inequality `abs_finset_sum_mul_le_sqrt` bounds the partial sum of `|L.levelInner a n|` by the product of `Real.sqrt (∑ n ∈ Finset.range M, (L.levelNorm n / w n)^2)` and `Real.sqrt (∑ n ∈ Finset.range M, (w n * a.levelNorm n)^2)`. Using `(L.levelNorm n)^2 = L.levelNormSq n` and `(a.levelNorm n)^2 = a.levelNormSq n`, these two finite sums inside the square roots equal the partial sums of `L.levelNormSq n / (w n)^2` and `(w n)^2 * a.levelNormSq n`, which are bounded above by their respective infinite series via `Summable.sum_le_tsum` under `MemL2w w L` and `MemL2wInv w a`; monotonicity of `Real.sqrt` and multiplication of nonnegative reals therefore yield `∑ n ∈ Finset.range M, |L.levelInner a n| ≤ norm2w w L * norm2wInv w a` uniformly in `M`. From this uniform partial-sum bound, `summable_of_sum_range_le` establishes absolute summability of `n ↦ L.levelInner a n`, and combining `abs_tsum_le_of_summable_abs` with `tsum_le_of_sum_range_le` gives `|dualPairing L a| ≤ norm2w w L * norm2wInv w a`.

Applying this general estimate to `L = ℓ` immediately yields the full pairing bound. For each `N : ℕ`, rewriting `(projGt N ℓ).levelNormSq n / (w n)^2` via `projGt.levelNormSq` shows that each term either equals `ℓ.levelNormSq n / (w n)^2` (when `N < n`) or `0` (when `n ≤ N`), and in both cases is nonnegative and dominated by `ℓ.levelNormSq n / (w n)^2`. Comparison (`Summable.of_nonneg_of_le`) against `hℓ` gives `MemL2w w (projGt N ℓ)`, and applying the general Cauchy–Schwarz pairing bound to `L = projGt N ℓ` gives the tail pairing estimate.
