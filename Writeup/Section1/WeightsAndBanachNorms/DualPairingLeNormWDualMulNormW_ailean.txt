**Theorem** (`dualPairing_le_normWDual_mul_normW`). For a weight `w : Weight` and elements `ℓ, a : ExtTensorAlgebra d` satisfying `MemTwDual w ℓ` and `MemTw w a`, the sequence of absolute levelwise inner products `n ↦ |ℓ.levelInner a n|` is summable, and the dual pairing satisfies `|dualPairing ℓ a| ≤ normWDual w ℓ * normW w a`.

Proof. For each level `n : ℕ`, strict positivity of `w n` converts the supremum bound `ℓ.levelNorm n / w n ≤ normWDual w ℓ` (`normWDual.levelNorm_div_le`) into `ℓ.levelNorm n ≤ normWDual w ℓ * w n`. Combining the levelwise Cauchy–Schwarz inequality `|ℓ.levelInner a n| ≤ ℓ.levelNorm n * a.levelNorm n` (`ExtTensorAlgebra.levelInner.abs_le`) with this bound and nonnegativity of `a.levelNorm n` gives the pointwise majorant `|ℓ.levelInner a n| ≤ normWDual w ℓ * (w n * a.levelNorm n)`. Since `MemTw w a` asserts summability of `n ↦ w n * a.levelNorm n`, multiplying on the left by `normWDual w ℓ` yields a summable dominating series, so `n ↦ |ℓ.levelInner a n|` is summable by comparison of nonnegative series (`Summable.of_nonneg_of_le`). Finally, the triangle inequality for absolutely summable series (`abs_tsum_le_of_summable_abs`), termwise comparison of `tsum`s, and factoring out the constant multiplier (`tsum_mul_left`) bound `|dualPairing ℓ a|` by `normWDual w ℓ * normW w a`.
