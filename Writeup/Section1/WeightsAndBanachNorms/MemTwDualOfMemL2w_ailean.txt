**Lemma** (`memTwDual_of_memL2w`). For a dimension `d : ℕ`, a weight `w : Weight`, and an extended tensor `ℓ : ExtTensorAlgebra d` belonging to the weighted Hilbert dual space (`MemL2w w ℓ`), `ℓ` also belongs to the weighted Banach dual (`MemTwDual w ℓ`) and satisfies the norm comparison `normWDual w ℓ ≤ norm2w w ℓ`.

Proof. Fix a level `n : ℕ`. Using `(ℓ.levelNorm n)^2 = ℓ.levelNormSq n` (`ExtTensorAlgebra.levelNorm.sq`), the squared ratio `(ℓ.levelNorm n / w n)^2` equals `ℓ.levelNormSq n / (w n)^2`. Since `ℓ` is summable in `MemL2w w ℓ` and every term `ℓ.levelNormSq k / (w k)^2` is nonnegative, the single level-`n` summand is bounded above by the full infinite series `∑' k, ℓ.levelNormSq k / (w k)^2` via `Summable.le_tsum`. Because `ℓ.levelNorm n / w n` is nonnegative (by nonnegativity of `levelNorm` and strict positivity of `w n`), taking square roots on both sides recovers `ℓ.levelNorm n / w n` on the left (`Real.sqrt_sq`) and yields `ℓ.levelNorm n / w n ≤ norm2w w ℓ` on the right (`Real.sqrt_le_sqrt`). This uniform bound exhibits `norm2w w ℓ` as an upper bound for the range of `n ↦ ℓ.levelNorm n / w n`, establishing `MemTwDual w ℓ`, and passing to the conditional supremum via `ciSup_le` gives `normWDual w ℓ ≤ norm2w w ℓ`.
