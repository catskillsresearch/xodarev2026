**Theorem** (`proposition_3_11_sharpness_of_H1`). For every `α : ℝ` with `0 < α`: (1) for every weight `w : Weight` satisfying `HypH2 w`, `superfactorialFunctional α` fails hypothesis H1 (`¬ HypH1 w (superfactorialFunctional α)`); and (2) for every `x : ℝ` with `1 ≤ α * |x|`, the series `n ↦ ((n.factorial : ℝ) * α ^ n) * (x ^ n / (n.factorial : ℝ))` is not summable.

Proof. Both parts are proved by contradiction. For the first claim, assume `hw : HypH2 w` and `h1 : HypH1 w (superfactorialFunctional α)`. Since `α > 0`, `α⁻¹ > 0`, so `hw.subfactorial (α⁻¹)` gives summability of `n ↦ w n * ((α⁻¹) ^ n / (n.factorial : ℝ))`, whose general term therefore tends to `0` as `n → ∞` (`Summable.tendsto_atTop_zero`). Likewise `h1.mem_l2w` implies that `(superfactorialFunctional α).levelNormSq n / (w n)^2` tends to `0`. Hence the product `(w n * ((α⁻¹) ^ n / (n.factorial : ℝ)))^2 * ((superfactorialFunctional α).levelNormSq n / (w n)^2)` also converges to `0`. However, substituting `superfactorialFunctional.levelNormSq` (`((n.factorial : ℝ) * α ^ n)^2`) and clearing denominators using `w n ≠ 0`, `(n.factorial : ℝ) ≠ 0`, and `α ≠ 0` simplifies this sequence identically to the constant `1`, contradicting uniqueness of limits in `ℝ` (`tendsto_nhds_unique` and `one_ne_zero`).

For the second claim, suppose `1 ≤ α * |x|` and the series is summable. Because `(n.factorial : ℝ) ≠ 0` for every `n`, algebraic cancellation simplifies the `n`-th term `((n.factorial : ℝ) * α ^ n) * (x ^ n / (n.factorial : ℝ))` to `(α * x) ^ n`. Summability of the geometric series `n ↦ (α * x) ^ n` implies `|α * x| < 1` (`summable_geometric_iff_norm_lt_one`), which rewrites via `α > 0` as `α * |x| < 1`, contradicting `1 ≤ α * |x|`.
