**Structure** (`HypH1`). *Hypothesis H1* for a weight $w$ and a coefficient series $\ell$ in the $(d + 1)$-letter extended tensor algebra has these properties:

- The series $\ell$ belongs to the weighted square-summable space associated with $w$ (`MemL2w`), meaning that $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \|\ell_n\|^2 / w(n)^2 < \infty$.
