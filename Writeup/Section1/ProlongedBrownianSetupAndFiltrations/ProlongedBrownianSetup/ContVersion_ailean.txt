For a process `X : ℝ → Ω → ℝ`, `contVersion X t ω` is the limit of `X (⌊t 2ⁿ⌋₊ / 2ⁿ) ω` as `n → ∞` (Lean's `limUnder` junk value if the limit does not exist). It only reads `X` at times `≤ t`, so it preserves adaptedness, and it agrees with `X t ω` whenever `X (·) ω` is continuous at `t` (`contVersion_eq_of_continuousAt`). Iterated integrals are built level by level from their continuous versions, as for the paper's continuous signature `Ŵ`. Key declarations include definitions `dyadicFloor`, `contVersion`; lemmas `dyadicFloor_nonneg`, `dyadicFloor_le`, `sub_dyadicFloor_le`, `tendsto_dyadicFloor`, `measurable_contVersion`, `contVersion_eq_of_tendsto`. The Lean source is `ProlongedBrownianSetup/ContVersion.lean`.
