If `𝔼(X_v - X_u)⁸ ≤ C (v - u)⁴` on `[0, T]`, then (Kolmogorov–Chentsov) almost surely the left-dyadic continuous version `contVersion X` agrees on `[0, T]` with a modification having continuous paths. Hence `contVersion X` has almost surely continuous paths on `[0, T]` and `contVersion X t = X t` almost surely for each `t ∈ [0, T]` (`contVersion_kolmogorov`).
