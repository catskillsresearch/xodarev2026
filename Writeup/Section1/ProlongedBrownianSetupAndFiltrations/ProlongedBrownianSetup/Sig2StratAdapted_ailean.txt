Proves `Measurable[(pathFiltration B).σAlg T]` for signature coordinates on `[0,s]` with `s ≤ T`. The filtration is packaged as an `IndexedFiltration` so the σ-algebra is never a bare `MeasurableSpace` variable (which would compete with the ambient instance on `MultiBrownian`). Key declarations include definitions `pathFiltration`; lemmas `measurable_W_pathFiltration`, `measurable_wnnIncr_pathFiltration`, `dyadic_time_mem_Icc`, `measurable_lebRiemann_wnnIncr_pathFiltration`, `measurable_lebIntegral_wnnIncrement_pathFiltration`, `measurable_itoIntegral_timeIntegrand_pathFiltration`.
