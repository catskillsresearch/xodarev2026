Paper Appendix (“Detailed Stratonovich–Itô conversion”): for each word `I`, \[ S^I_t = I^I_t + \sum_{J:|J|<|I|} a_{I,J}\, I^J_t, \] with combinatorial coefficients `a_{I,J}` from non-overlapping adjacent equal space-letter bracket contractions (each replaced by a time letter `0`, weight `(1/2)^|Γ|`). Level `≤ 1` has no correction (Stratonovich = Itô). The dual/generator shadow of the leading double-space contraction is `stratonovichHalfLaplacian` (used in Riccati). Key declarations include definitions `contractions`, `stratItoCoeff`, `stratItoCorrection`, `stratWordIntegral`, `stratSig2`; lemmas `stratItoCorrection_of_length_le_one`, `stratWordIntegral_empty`, `stratWordIntegral_time`, `stratWordIntegral_space`, `measurable_stratItoCorrection`, `measurable_stratWordIntegral`.
