`B.shift s₀` is the Brownian motion `r ↦ W_{s₀ + r} - W_{s₀}` (weak Markov property, Mathlib's `IsBrownianReal.shift`). Almost surely, simultaneously for all words and all upper times, the Itô word integrals of `B` started at `s` equal those of `B.shift s` started at `0` (`ae_itoWordIntegral_shift`). All `0`-start results (moments, continuity, dynamics) therefore transfer to `s`-start word integrals, as needed for Chen's identity. Key declarations include definitions `MultiBrownian.shift`; lemmas `wnnIncr_shift`, `W_shift`, `itoWordStep_of_le`, `contVersion_itoWordStep_self`, `ae_itoWordStep_eq`; theorems `ae_itoWordIntegral_shift`. The Lean source is `ProlongedBrownianSetup/ShiftedWordIntegral.lean`.
