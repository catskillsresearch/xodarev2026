Packs `paperSig2` (iterated Stratonovich integrals of `X = (t, W_t)` in the paper's word order) into `ProlongedBrownianSetup`. Chen, shuffle, factorial moments and the signature dynamics are the theorems `paperSig2_*` of `PaperSig`, `PaperSigDynamics` and `PaperSigChen`. Key declarations include definitions `prolongedBrownianSetup_stratonovich_of_brownian`. The Lean source is `ProlongedBrownianSetup/StratonovichOfBM.lean`.
