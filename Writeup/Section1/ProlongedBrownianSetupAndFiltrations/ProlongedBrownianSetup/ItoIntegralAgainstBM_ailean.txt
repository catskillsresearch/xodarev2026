Uses `Wnn` (Mathlib `ℝ≥0`-time BM) and closed-form Itô identities for length-2 words. Lebesgue integrals of BM paths are defined as limits of dyadic Riemann sums (measurable in ω). Key declarations include definitions `wnnIncr`, `lebRiemann_wnnIncr`, `lebIntegral_wnnIncrement`, `lebIntegral_timeIntegrand`, `itoIntegral_timeIntegrand`, `itoIntegral_wnnIncrement_diag`; lemmas `measurable_wnnIncr`, `measurable_lebRiemann_wnnIncr`, `measurable_lebIntegral_wnnIncrement`, `measurable_itoIntegral_timeIntegrand`, `measurable_itoIntegral_wnnIncrement_diag`.
