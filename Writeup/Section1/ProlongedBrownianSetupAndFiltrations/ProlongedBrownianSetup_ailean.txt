**Structure** (`ProlongedBrownianSetup`). A *prolonged Brownian setup* on a probability space $(\Omega, \mathcal F, \mathbb{P})$ in dimension $d \in \mathbb{N}$ on a horizon $[0, T]$ has these properties:

- The terminal horizon is strictly positive, $T > 0$.
- A $d$-dimensional process $W : \mathbb{R} \times \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$ starts at the origin ($W_0^i(\omega) = 0$ for every $\omega \in \Omega$ and coordinate $i \in \{0, \dots, d-1\}$) and has measurable coordinates $\omega \mapsto W_t^i(\omega)$ at every time $t \in \mathbb{R}$.
- A two-parameter Stratonovich signature $\widehat{W}_{s,t}(\omega)$ takes values in the extended tensor algebra $T((\mathbb{R}^{d+1}))$ over the time-augmented alphabet of size $d + 1$, with measurable word coordinates $\omega \mapsto \langle e_I, \widehat{W}_{s,t}(\omega)\rangle$ for all $s, t \in \mathbb{R}$ and words $I$.
- Its low-degree coordinates recover the scalar unit and the increments of $(t, W_t)$: for all $s, t \in \mathbb{R}$ and $\omega \in \Omega$, the empty-word coordinate is $\langle e_{\emptyset}, \widehat{W}_{s,t}(\omega)\rangle = 1$, the time-letter coordinate is $\langle e_{(0)}, \widehat{W}_{s,t}(\omega)\rangle = t - s$, and each spatial-letter coordinate ($i \in \{0, \dots, d-1\}$) is $\langle e_{(i+1)}, \widehat{W}_{s,t}(\omega)\rangle = W_t^i(\omega) - W_s^i(\omega)$.
- The signature from time $0$ is adapted to the natural filtration $(\mathcal F_t^W)$ generated by $W$: for every $t \in \mathbb{R}$, every $s \in [0, t]$, and every word $I$, $\omega \mapsto \langle e_I, \widehat{W}_{0,s}(\omega)\rangle$ is $\mathcal F_t^W$-measurable.
- Chen's multiplicative relation holds along $[0, T]$: whenever $0 \le s \le t \le T$, $\widehat{W}_{0,t}(\omega) = \widehat{W}_{0,s}(\omega) \otimes \widehat{W}_{s,t}(\omega)$ for all $\omega \in \Omega$.
- The Stratonovich shuffle identity holds on $[0, T]$: for every $t \in [0, T]$, all words $I, J$ on $d + 1$ letters, and all $\omega \in \Omega$, $\langle e_I, \widehat{W}_{0,t}(\omega)\rangle \langle e_J, \widehat{W}_{0,t}(\omega)\rangle = \langle e_I \mathbin{\sqcup\!\sqcup} e_J, \widehat{W}_{0,t}(\omega)\rangle$.
- Level norms obey factorial moment bounds: for every $p \ge 1$ there exist constants $C_p, R_p \ge 0$ such that for any nonnegative sequence $(w_n)_{n \ge 0}$ with $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} w_n C_p R_p^n / n! < \infty$ and any $t \in [0, T]$, the weighted series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} w_n \|\widehat{W}_{0,t}(\omega)_n\|$ converges for all $\omega \in \Omega$, its $p$-th power is $\mathbb{P}$-integrable, and
$$\int_{\Omega} \left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} w_n \|\widehat{W}_{0,t}(\omega)_n\|\right)^p d\mathbb{P}(\omega) \le \left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} w_n C_p \frac{R_p^n}{n!}\right)^p.$$
