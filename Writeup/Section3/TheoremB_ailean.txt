**Theorem** (`theorem_B`). Under a `SignatureVolatilityModel`, `ProlongedBrownianSetup`, `ItoCalculusKit`, and `SignatureRiccatiKit`, there is a `SignaturePriceSolution` that simultaneously: (i) is a square-integrable martingale relative to `setup.signatureFiltration` on admissible time windows, (ii) makes `AdmissibleGainsUnderP ℙ` satisfy NFLVR, (iii) yields a nonempty `IsSpecialSemimartingale` witness for every coordinate process `t ↦ (setup.W_hat t ω).coeff I` of the prolonged signature, and (iv) for every initial tensor `u₀` in the free algebra, whenever a `ClassicalRiccatiSolution` exists for `ricKit.R_op` and `u₀.1`, there is a solution `rSol` whose exponential dual-pricing formula holds: for `t ∈ [0, M.T]`, the conditional expectation of `exp(dualPairing u₀.1 (setup.W_hat M.T ω))` given `signatureFiltration` at `t` equals almost surely `exp(dualPairing (rSol.ψ (M.T - t)) (setup.W_hat t ω))`, with integrability at horizon `M.T`.

Proof. Apply `theorem_A` to obtain a price solution `sol`; discard the unpacked uniqueness data. Return `sol` bundled with four properties. Martingale and `L^2` integrability are `sol.is_martingale` from the Theorem A package. NFLVR is `nflvr_of_martingale_measure ℙ` applied to admissible gains. For each word `I`, `ricKit.coord_special_semimartingale I` supplies the special semimartingale witness as an explicit `Nonempty` proof. For the Riccati duality clause, assume `hne : Nonempty (ClassicalRiccatiSolution …)` for given `u₀`; the goal is discharged by `(ricKit.affine_riccati_duality u₀).mpr hne`, which is exactly the existential Riccati solution with the stated conditional-expectation identity and integrability on `Icc 0 M.T`.
