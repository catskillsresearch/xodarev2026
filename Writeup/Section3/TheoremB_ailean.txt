**Theorem** (`theorem_B_mp`). Under a `SignatureVolatilityModel`, a Brownian driver, and the analytic hypothesis H3, there is a `SignaturePriceSolution` that is a square-integrable martingale on the signature filtration, together with Delbaen–Schachermayer NFLVR for the cone `BoundedBelowGains` of admissible strategies whose wealth is uniformly bounded from below, applied to a continuous version `sigPriceCont` of the packaged price (equal to it almost surely at each time), a nonempty `IsSpecialSemimartingale` witness for every coordinate of the prolonged signature, and the forward affine–Riccati implication: whenever a classical Riccati solution exists, the exponential dual-pricing formula holds.

Proof. Apply `theorem_A` to obtain a packaged price solution. Martingale and $L^2$ integrability are those of the Theorem A package. The continuous version `sigPriceCont` has almost surely continuous paths and agrees almost surely at each time with the packaged Doléans exponential; Delbaen–Schachermayer NFLVR for its bounded-below admissible gains is `satisfiesNFLVR_DS_sigPriceCont`. Special semimartingale coordinates and the forward Riccati implication are supplied by the signature Riccati kit, as before.
