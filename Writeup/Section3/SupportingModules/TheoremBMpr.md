Paper: Xodarev Theorem B. Paper Theorem B claimed, under H1–H3, a martingale price, NFLVR, special-semimartingale signature coordinates, and the affine / Riccati iff for every finitely supported `u₀` with finite signature transform. The first three clauses and the `→` direction of the iff are true (`theorem_B_mp`). The `←` direction is false as a universal statement, so the full conjunction is false.
