**Theorem** (`remark_2_19_black_scholes_riccati`). For real `c₁`, let `idx1` be the successor of `0` in `Fin 1`, hence the single nontrivial letter in `Letter 2`, and let `u₀` be `c₁` times the basis word `[idx1]` in `ExtTensorAlgebra 2`. Then the empty-word coefficient of `riccatiOpBounded 1 u₀` equals `(1/2) * c₁^2`.

Proof. After binding `idx1` and `u₀`, identify `idx1` with `(1 : Fin 2)`. The empty coefficient of `rightShift 0 u₀` is zero: unfolding definitions, it is `c₁` times an indicator that the empty word appended with `[idx1]` equals `[idx1]`, which is false for the shift at index `0` once `idx1` is known. Likewise `(stratonovichHalfLaplacian u₀).coeff []` vanishes: the half-Laplacian sum over `Fin 1` reduces to a single term whose word concatenation cannot match `[idx1]`.

For the shuffle term, unfold `shuffleFinite (rightShift idx1 u₀) (rightShift idx1 u₀) 1` and split finite sums over word lengths. For length pairs `(2,·)` and `(·,1)` with words from `wordsOfLength`, every contribution has a zero coefficient from `rightShift idx1 u₀` because appending `idx1` to a nontrivial length-1 word cannot reproduce `[idx1]`; length contradictions come from `congrArg List.length`. After these sums vanish, only the `(0,0)` shuffle pairing survives. Simplifying `wordsOfLength`, `rightShift`, `basisWord`, `shuffleBasis`, and `shuffleWords` at `idx1`, and normalizing with `ring_nf`, shows the surviving empty coefficient is `u₀.coeff [idx1]^2`. Direct computation gives `u₀.coeff [idx1] = c₁`, hence the square is `c₁^2`.

Finally, `riccatiOpBounded` at truncation level `1` adds the empty coefficients of the right shift at `0`, the Stratonovich half-Laplacian, and one half times the shuffle sum over `Fin 1`. The first two are zero, `Fin.sum_univ_one` leaves the shuffle term at index `idx1`, and scaling by `1/2` turns `c₁^2` into `(1/2) * c₁^2`.
