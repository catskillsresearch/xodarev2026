**Definition** (`SatisfiesNFLVR`). Relative to a set of admissible terminal gains, we say that a probability measure $\mathbb P$ satisfies *no free lunch with vanishing risk* (NFLVR) if the following implication holds: whenever $(G_n)$ is a sequence of admissible gains, $f \ge 0$ is an integrable nonnegative limit, each $G_n$ has nonpositive mean and is integrable, the negative part of $G_n$ is eventually uniformly bounded below, and $G_n(\omega) \to f(\omega)$ for $\mathbb P$-almost every $\omega$, then $f = 0$ almost surely under $\mathbb P$.
