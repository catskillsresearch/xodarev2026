`riccatiOp u` is the coefficientwise-exact version of `riccatiOpBounded`: the shuffle square at output word `K` is truncated at `|K|`, which loses nothing because a shuffle of words of lengths `m, n` only has words of length `m + n`. On the finitely supported core `𝒟_fin` it agrees with `riccatiOpBounded N` for every support bound `N`. Key declarations include lemmas `length_of_mem_shuffleWords`, `shuffleBasis_coeff_eq_zero`, `coeff_finset_sum`, `shuffleFinite_coeff_eq_min`, `shuffleFinite_coeff_eq_length`; definitions `riccatiOp`; theorems `riccatiOp_eq_bounded`.
