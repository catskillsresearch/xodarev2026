Take a classical Riccati solution `ψ` (coefficientwise integral equation, uniformly bounded `T_w` norm). This file proves that the exponent of the candidate affine transform, `Y_t = ⟨ψ(T − t), Ŵ_t⟩ = ∑_n ⟨ψ(T − t)^{(n)}, Ŵ_t^{(n)}⟩`, is well defined and continuous: * every coefficient `t ↦ ψ_I(t)` is continuous on `[0, T]` (`continuousOn_coeff`); * almost surely the level series converges absolutely, uniformly in `t ∈ [0, T]`, and `Y` is continuous on `[0, T]` (`ae_riccatiExponent_summable_continuousOn`). The uniform majorant is `ae_levelNorm_bound_paperSig2`, the Kolmogorov grid-supremum bound on the signature levels. Key declarations include lemmas `continuousOn_coeff`, `levelNorm_le_normW`; definitions `exponent`; theorems `ae_exponent_summable_continuousOn`. The Lean source is `SignatureRiccatiKit/RiccatiExpProcess.lean`.
