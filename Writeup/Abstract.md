# Abstract

We formalize, in Lean 4 and Mathlib, the structural theory of signature volatility models developed by Akmal Xodarev [@xodarev2026]. We prove those claims of the paper that hold, at the exact strength of the corresponding Lean theorems, and we refute those that do not, by Lean negations of those claims and nothing stronger.

Under hypotheses H1--H3, a strictly positive price $S$ exists and is unique up to modification among adapted strong solutions of the linear equation $dS = S\,\sigma\,dW$. In the unwrapped signature-price predicate, almost every path of $S$ is continuous on $[0,T]$, and at each time $t\in[0,T]$ the process agrees almost surely with the Doléans exponential of the signature volatility. Under the same hypotheses $S$ is a martingale on the signature filtration, the market satisfies Delbaen--Schachermayer NFLVR for uniformly bounded-below admissible strategies whose integrand $HS\sigma$ lies in $\mathcal{F}^W$-predictable $L^2$ and whose wealth is a continuous genuine $L^2$ Itô integral against $W^0$ (narrower than the paper's local class $\int H^2\,d\langle S\rangle<\infty$ almost surely), the signature coordinates are special semimartingales, and the Riccati conjunct is the immediate implication that a classical Riccati solution satisfying the exponential dual formula yields a classical Riccati solution (recorded as stated, not a substantive forward pricing result).

The converse duality, the paper's full Theorem B conjunction, the unconditional completeness-depth equivalence (for an arbitrary finite strike set), the paper's Black--Scholes depth claim (Proposition 58), and the residual hypothesis of Theorem D fail at the stated strength. These completeness conclusions are not a counterexample to Theorem 56 in its finite-polynomial subcase $K(N_S^\ast)<\infty$ with a sufficiently rich strike set: the Black--Scholes witness has $N_S^\ast=\infty$ under Definition 55. The paper's literal NFLVR (no wealth bound) and the terminal-only bound of Section 4.6 likewise fail in the Black--Scholes market. The geometric-grid doubling strategy and the distinction among those three NFLVR wordings, as well as the scaled-tensor disproof of the converse Riccati duality, are due to Gemini Carbon.
