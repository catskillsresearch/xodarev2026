# Introduction

This formalization reconstructs Xodarev's structural theory of signature volatility models [@xodarev2026] in Lean 4, using Mathlib. The development follows the paper's mathematical order: the weighted tensor algebra and the prolonged Brownian signature; Theorems A--D; and the canonical models (Black--Scholes, first-order Brownian volatility, Heston level 2, Quintic OU, and the geometric signature).

The paper separates three filtrations: the Brownian filtration $\mathcal F^W$, the prolonged-signature filtration $\mathcal F^{\widehat W}$ (equal to $\mathcal F^W$ after time augmentation), and the price filtration $\mathcal F^S$, which is generally smaller. Completeness (Theorem C) and the GKW residual (Theorem D) are stated on $\mathcal F^S$, not as claims on the Brownian filtration. The Lean development mirrors that split: `signatureFiltration`, `brownianFiltration`, and `priceFiltration`, together with the equality of the first two after time augmentation.

The paper presents Theorems A--D as a chain: well-posedness of the stochastic exponential (A) feeds NFLVR and Riccati duality (B); completeness depth (C) is stated on the price filtration; and that depth feeds the residual split in the hedging-error theorem (D). Lean proves Theorem A and the GKW projection of Theorem D at the strength recorded in the table of main results. It proves the forward fragment of Theorem B, including Delbaen--Schachermayer NFLVR for admissible gains whose wealth is uniformly bounded from below. It refutes the paper's full Theorem B conjunction, the converse affine/Riccati duality, Theorem C, Proposition 58, the residual hypothesis of Theorem D (for Black--Scholes), and Lemmas 77 and 78, each at the exact strength of the corresponding negation. The paper's literal NFLVR and the terminal-only lower bound are likewise refuted, for Black--Scholes, by a geometric-grid doubling strategy. Every compared claim is either proved or refuted at the stated strength.
