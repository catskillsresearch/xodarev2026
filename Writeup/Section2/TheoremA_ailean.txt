**Theorem** (`theorem_A`). Given a `SignatureVolatilityModel` `M`, a `ProlongedBrownianSetup`, and an `ItoCalculusKit`, there exists a `SignaturePriceSolution` `sol` such that any adapted candidate price process `S_tilde` that satisfies the Itô SDE with coefficient `S_tilde · sigma` on `[0, M.T]` almost surely must agree with `sol.S` at every time in that interval, almost surely.

Proof. From `ito.novikov_doob_l2_martingale`, using `M.w`, `M.h2`, `M.ℓ`, `M.h1`, initial price `M.s₀`, and strict positivity `M.hs₀`, obtain a square-integrable martingale property `hmart`, a uniform `L^2` integrability witness `hsup`, and a quadratic-variation integrability hypothesis `hquad` for the volatility-driven exponential construction.

Define `σ` to be `SignatureVolatilityModel.sigma M setup` and define the candidate solution by `S t ω = M.s₀ * ito.doleansExp σ t ω`. At time zero, `S 0 ω = M.s₀` follows from the definitions of `doleansExp`, vanishing of `itoInt` at zero, and vanishing quadratic variation at zero. For each fixed `t`, measurability of `S t` is proved by rewriting `S t` as `M.s₀` times an exponential of `ito.itoInt σ t` minus one half times `ito.quadVar σ t`, then composing measurability of the exponential with measurability of the Itô integral and quadratic variation (`itoInt_meas`, `quadVar_meas`). Adaptedness to `setup.signatureFiltration` at times `s ≤ t` is the same exponential form, using `itoInt_adapted` and `quadVar_adapted` instead of global measurability. Strict positivity uses `M.hs₀` and `Real.exp_pos`.

The Doléans representation and SDE identity `S t ω = M.s₀ + ito.itoInt (fun s ω' ↦ S s ω' * σ s ω') t ω` on `Icc 0 M.T`, almost surely, come from `ito.doleans_sde` applied to `M.s₀`, `σ`, and `hquad`. Package `S` into a `SignaturePriceSolution` with the fields above, `doleans_repr` by definitional simp, `solves_sde := hsde`, `sup_l2_integrable := hsup`, and `is_martingale := hmart`.

For uniqueness, let `S_tilde` satisfy the SDE hypothesis `hS_tilde`. Apply `ito.stopped_l2_localisation` with `M.w`, `M.ℓ`, `M.s₀`, the candidate `S_tilde`, the constructed `S`, `hS_tilde`, and the established SDE for `S`, obtaining a nonnegative stopping exhaustion `⟨f, hf_cont, hf_nonneg, hf_bound, hexhaust⟩`. It suffices to show that for each index `k` and each `t` in the interval, `S_tilde t` agrees with `S t`. This is exactly `gronwall_zero_of_continuousOn` with the time horizon `M.hT.le`, using `sq_nonneg _` as the quadratic forcing template, the continuity `hf_cont k`, nonnegativity `hf_nonneg k`, and the bound `hf_bound k` supplied by localization. Combining the existence package with this uniqueness clause yields the theorem.
