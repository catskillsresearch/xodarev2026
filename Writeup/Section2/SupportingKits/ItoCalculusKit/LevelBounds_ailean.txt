For every non-empty word `I` and every `q ≥ 2`, the Itô iterated integral `X_u = itoWordIntegral B 0 u · I` on `[0, T]` is adapted, a.s. continuous, in `L^{2q}`, with * `‖X_u‖_{2q}² ≤ wordConst q I · u ^ wordExp I`, and * `‖X_v - X_u‖_{2q}² ≤ L (v - u)` for some `L`. A time letter adds `2` to the (squared-norm) exponent and divides the constant by `(q e + 1)^{1/q}`; an Itô letter adds `1` and divides by `e + 1` (`levelBound_itoWordIntegral`).
