For `q ≥ 2` and a process `H` with almost surely continuous paths on `[0, T]` and `H_u ∈ L^{2q}`: * the uniform-grid `lebIntegral_process H 0 t` equals the pathwise integral `∫₀ᵗ H` a.s.; * `‖∫₀ᵗ H‖_{2q}² ≤ A t^{e+2} (q e + 1)^{-1/q}` when `‖H_u‖_{2q}² ≤ A u^e`; * `‖∫ᵤᵛ H‖_{2q}² ≤ M (v - u)²` when `‖H_u‖_{2q}² ≤ M`. Both bounds come from the discrete Jensen inequality on Riemann sums and Fatou.
