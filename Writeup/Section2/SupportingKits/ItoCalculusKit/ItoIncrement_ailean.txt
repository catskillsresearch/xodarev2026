A uniform-grid Itô–Riemann sum on `[0, a]` is, pathwise, the limit of Riemann sums of the corresponding step integrand along fine uniform grids of a larger interval `[0, v]` (`tendsto_fineStepSum`). This puts sums over *different* horizons on a common grid, so the fourth-moment martingale-transform bound applies to their difference. Key declarations include definitions `stepFun`; lemmas `sum_range_ite_wnnIncr`, `tendsto_ceil_mul`, `stepFun_eq`, `floor_mul_mem`, `lintegral_pow_four_le_of_tendsto`, `integral_pow_four_le_of_tendsto`; theorems `tendsto_fineStepSum`.
