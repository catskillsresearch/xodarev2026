For the uniform grids `t·j/(n+1)` used by `itoRiemann_process`, any block of centred squared increments `∑_{j ∈ sₙ} ((ΔW_j)² - t/(n+1))`, `sₙ ⊆ {0,…,n}`, converges to `0` **almost surely along the full sequence** `n → ∞` (fourth moments are `O(n⁻²)`, hence summable). Countably many blocks give the pathwise Föllmer hypothesis `ae_tendsto_qvBlocks`. Key declarations include definitions `qvFourthConst`, `qvIncr`; lemmas `sub_pow_four_le`, `memLp_sq_sub_gaussianReal`, `integral_sq_sub_gaussianReal`, `integral_sq_sub_pow_four_gaussianReal`, `hasLaw_wnnIncr`, `measurable_qvIncr`.
