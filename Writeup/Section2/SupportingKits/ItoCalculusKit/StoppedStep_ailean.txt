Abstract form of one grid step of `Φ(t) = E[I_t² e^{-Q_t}]` for the difference `I` of two solutions of the linear equation `dI = I σ dW`, with `Q = ∫ σ²`. With `a = e^{-Q_u} ∈ [0, 1]`, `ΔQ = Q_v - Q_u`, `G = I_u σ_u` and a step of length `s²`: `E[I_v² a e^{-ΔQ}] - E[I_u² a] ≤ C₃ s³ + C₄ s⁴`. The `E[a G² ΔW²] = s² E[a I_u² σ_u²]` terms cancel; all other terms are `O(s³)` by Cauchy–Schwarz.
