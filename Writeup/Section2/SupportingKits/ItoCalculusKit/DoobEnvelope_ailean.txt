If `X` satisfies `X_q = 𝔼[X_T | 𝓕_q]` at the dyadic points of `[0, T]`, `X_T ∈ L²`, and on the dyadic points `X` agrees almost surely with a process `X̃` with continuous paths on `[0, T]`, then Doob's `L²` maximal inequality on the dyadic grids and Fatou give a measurable `L²` envelope of `|X̃|` on all of `[0, T]` (`exists_envelope_of_dyadic_martingale`). Key declarations include definitions `gridMaxAbs`; lemmas `measurable_gridMaxAbs`, `le_gridMaxAbs`, `gridMaxAbs_nonneg`, `gridMaxAbs_mono`, `dyadicGrid_le`, `dyadicGrid_mem`; theorems `exists_envelope_of_dyadic_martingale`. The Lean source is `ItoCalculusKit/DoobEnvelope.lean`.
