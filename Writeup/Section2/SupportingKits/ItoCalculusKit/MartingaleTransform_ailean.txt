For a partition `τ 0 ≤ τ 1 ≤ …` and integrand values `h j ∈ L⁴` that are measurable for the **joint** path filtration `𝓕_{τ j}` of all coordinates (e.g. `h j = σ_{τ j}` for a signature volatility), the discrete stochastic integral against one driver coordinate satisfies `𝔼[(∑_{j<m} h_j (W^k_{τ(j+1)} - W^k_{τ j}))⁴] ≤ 16 · 𝔼[G⁴] · (∑_{j<m} √𝔼[h_j⁴] (τ(j+1) - τ j))²` (`integral_transform_pow_four_le`). This is the discrete Burkholder-type estimate behind almost-sure, full-sequence convergence of Itô–Riemann sums for adapted integrands; it combines `integral_sum_pow_four_le_of_orthogonal` with independence of driver increments from the joint past (`indepFun_pathFiltration_wnnIncr`).
