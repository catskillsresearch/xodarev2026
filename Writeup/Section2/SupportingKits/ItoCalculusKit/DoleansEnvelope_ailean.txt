At a time `t` where the kit Riemann sums converge, their limit is identified (along the subsequence `N_j`, simultaneously for all `t`, `SubseqUniform`) with the continuous version `Ĩ_t`; where they do not converge, `limUnder` returns the fixed junk value `junkReal`. Hence `Z_t ≤ max(exp(Ĩ_t - ½∫σ²), e^{junkReal})` for **every** `t ∈ [0, T]` almost surely, and Doob's `L²` inequality on the dyadic martingale `Z` (`DoobEnvelope`) bounds the first term. Key declarations include definitions `junkReal`; lemmas `limUnder_eq_junkReal`, `kitItoIntRaw_eq_limUnder`, `tendsto_subseqN_sub_one`; theorems `exists_envelope_kitDoleansExp_signatureSigma`.
