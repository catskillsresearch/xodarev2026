Sampling `gbm` on the dyadic grid of `[0, T]` with `2ⁿ` cells gives a discrete martingale (`condExp_gbm`), so Doob's `L²` maximal inequality bounds the grid maximum by `2 ‖gbm_T‖₂` uniformly in `n`. The grids are nested, so the grid maxima increase to a measurable envelope `gbmEnvelope`; Fatou puts it in `L²`, and path continuity makes it dominate `gbm_t` for every `t ∈ [0, T]` (`exists_gbm_envelope`). Key declarations include definitions `dyadicGrid`, `dyadicFiltration`, `gbmGridMax`; lemmas `dyadicGrid_nonneg`, `dyadicGrid_mono`, `dyadicGrid_last`, `dyadicGrid_succ`, `martingale_gbm_dyadic`, `measurable_gbmGridMax`. The Lean source is `ItoCalculusKit/GBMEnvelope.lean`.
