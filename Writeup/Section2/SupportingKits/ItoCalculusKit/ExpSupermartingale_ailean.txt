For an integrand `H` adapted to the path filtration, the discrete exponentials `exp (∑ q Hᵢ ΔWᵢ - q²/2 Hᵢ² Δtᵢ)` along the uniform grid have expectation exactly `1` (`lintegral_exp_unifStep_eq_one`; Gaussian increments independent of the past). By Fatou, any almost-sure limits `I` of the Itô–Riemann sums and `Q` of the Lebesgue sums of `H²` satisfy `E exp (q I - q²/2 Q) ≤ 1` (`lintegral_exp_ito_le_one`).
