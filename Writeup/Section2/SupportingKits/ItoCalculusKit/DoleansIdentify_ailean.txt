For the signature volatility under H1–H3 and `t ∈ [0, T]`, the left Itô–Riemann sums of `H = s₀ Z σ` on the uniform grids converge almost surely to `s₀ Z_t - s₀` (`ae_tendsto_unifRiemann_doleans`). Proof: `Z(τᵢ₊₁) = Z(τᵢ) e^{xᵢ}` with `xᵢ = σ(τᵢ) ΔWᵢ + aᵢ - ½ ∫_{τᵢ}^{τᵢ₊₁} σ²`, telescoping, and `tendsto_doleansError`, whose hypotheses are supplied by `DoleansInputs`, the grid Doob bound, continuity of `σ` and of the Brownian path, and almost-sure quadratic variation. Key declarations include lemmas `exists_modulus_of_continuousOn`, `kitDoleansExp_step`, `kitDoleansExp_signatureSigma_zero`; theorems `ae_tendsto_sum_wnnIncr_sq`, `ae_tendsto_unifRiemann_doleans`, `kitItoIntRaw_doleans_ae`, `integrable_kitItoIntRaw_doleans`.
