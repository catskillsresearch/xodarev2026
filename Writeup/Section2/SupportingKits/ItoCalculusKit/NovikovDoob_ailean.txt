Under H1–H3 and the strengthened continuous independent driver, the Doléans price is a square-integrable martingale on the signature filtration, with an a.e. L² envelope and pathwise `IntegrableOn σ²`. Analytic content: Novikov from H3 (`λ = 1/2`), Doob maximal inequality, and Gaussian signature moments of `σ` under `pathContinuous`. Key declarations include theorems `kit_novikov_doob_l2_martingale_of_driver`, `kit_novikov_doob_l2_martingale`; lemmas `kitDoleansExp_eq_gbm_of_const_on`, `kitDoleansExp_of_no_driver`. The Lean source is `ItoCalculusKit/NovikovDoob.lean`.
