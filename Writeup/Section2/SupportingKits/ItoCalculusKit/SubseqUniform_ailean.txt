Along `N_j = 2^{16 j}`, almost surely `R_{N_j}(t) → Ĩ_t` for **every** `t ∈ [0, T]`, where `Ĩ` is a continuous version of the raw Itô integral. The proof combines the eighth-moment rate (`RiemannRate8`), Borel–Cantelli on the grids `m T / N_j³`, and the pathwise `7/20`-Hölder bounds of `σ` and `W` (`HolderPath`), which control `R_N(t) - R_N(t')` deterministically. Key declarations include lemmas `abs_riemannSum_sub_le`, `subseqN_pos`, `subseqM_pos`, `subseqN_cast`, `subseqM_cast`, `subseqGrid_mem`; definitions `subseqN`, `subseqM`, `subseqGrid`.
