For an adapted `L⁴`-Hölder integrand the integrability gate of `kitItoInt` is open (the raw quadratic variation is integrable by Fatou, with the `limUnder` junk value on non-convergent paths), so `kitItoInt = kitItoIntRaw`. The raw integral is linear a.s. Consequently the difference `D = S₁ - S₂` of two kit strong solutions is `D_t = ∫₀ᵗ D σ dW` (raw) a.s. Key declarations include lemmas `HolderAdapted.sub`, `unifRiemann_sub`; theorems `integrable_kitQuadVarRaw_of_holderAdapted`, `kitItoInt_eq_raw_of_holderAdapted`, `kitItoIntRaw_sub_ae`, `holderAdapted_of_strongClass`, `stopped_diff_eq_raw`.
