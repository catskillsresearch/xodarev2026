For a `MultiBrownian` driver the increment `W^k_u - W^k_s` of **one** coordinate is independent of the **joint** path filtration `𝓕_s = σ(W^i_r : r ≤ s, i < d)` of **all** coordinates (`indep_wnnIncr_pathFiltration`). This is where the process-level independence `MultiBrownian.coordsIndep` is used: the weak Markov property of the coordinate `k` handles its own past, and independence of the coordinate *processes* handles the other coordinates' pasts (fixed-time independence of the coordinates would not suffice).
