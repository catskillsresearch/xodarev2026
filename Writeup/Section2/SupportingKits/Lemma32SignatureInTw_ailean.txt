**Theorem** (`lemma_3_2_signature_in_Tw`). Fix a probability space, a `ProlongedBrownianSetup`, a weight `w` satisfying `HypH2 w`, an exponent `p` with `1 ≤ p`, and a time `t` with `0 ≤ t ≤ T`. Then for every sample path `ω`, the prolonged signature `setup.W_hat t ω` lies in `MemTw w`, and the random variable `ω ↦ (normW w (setup.W_hat t ω))^p` is integrable with respect to `ℙ`.

Proof. From `setup.factorial_moment_control` at exponent `p`, obtain constants `C_p`, `R_p`, the hypothesis `hRp` that `R_p` is usable in the subfactorial estimate, and a control theorem `hctrl` relating `MemTw`, integrability, and a summable majorant series in `n`. First show that `Summable (fun n ↦ w n * (C_p * (R_p^n / n.factorial)))`. By extensionality and `ring`, this series equals `C_p` times `fun n ↦ w n * (R_p^n / n.factorial)`, which is summable by `HypH2.subfactorial_nonneg` applied to `hRp`, then multiplied on the left by `C_p`. Apply `hctrl` to `w`, using `fun n ↦ (w.pos n).le` for the coefficient inequalities, together with this summability and the time bounds `ht0`, `htT`. The conclusion unpacks into membership in `MemTw w` almost surely (actually pointwise here) and `p`-integrability of the weighted norm; the proof returns exactly this pair.
