**Theorem** (`corollary_3_6_sigma_well_defined`). For a `SignatureVolatilityModel` `M`, a matching `ProlongedBrownianSetup`, and `t` with `0 ≤ t ≤ M.T`, the volatility field `SignatureVolatilityModel.sigma M setup t ω` is absolutely bounded by `norm2w M.w M.ℓ * normW M.w (setup.W_hat t ω)` for every `ω`, and `ω ↦ (normW M.w (setup.W_hat t ω))^2` is integrable under `ℙ`.

Proof. Invoke `lemma_3_2_signature_in_Tw` at `p = 2` (via `1 ≤ 2`) using the model’s hypothesis `M.h2` and the same time window, obtaining that `setup.W_hat t ω` lies in `MemTw M.w` and that the squared weighted norm is integrable. From `M.h1.mem_l2w`, apply `memTwDual_of_memL2w` to get a dual element and a comparison `hDual_le` between `norm2w M.w M.ℓ` and the dual norm. For each `ω`, unfold `SignatureVolatilityModel.sigma` and use the dual-pairing bound `dualPairing_le_normWDual_mul_normW` evaluated on `hTw ω`, then transitively replace the dual norm by `norm2w` using `hDual_le` and multiply on the right by the nonnegative `normW M.w (setup.W_hat t ω)`. Integrability is the second component from Lemma 3.2.
