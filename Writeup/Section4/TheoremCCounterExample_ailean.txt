The unconditional form of the completeness-depth equivalence says that, for an arbitrary finite set of call strikes, the market of `S` plus those calls is complete on `ℱ^S_T` **iff** `N^*_S < ∞`. Theorem 56 of the source paper restricts that equivalence to the finite-polynomial subcase `K(N^*_S) < ∞` and a sufficiently rich strike set. The unrestricted equivalence, and the paper's separate claim that Black–Scholes has `N^*_S = 0`, fail: Black–Scholes is complete on `ℱ^S_T` (every `X ∈ L²(ℱ^S_T)` is `E X` plus a stochastic integral, by Brownian martingale representation, so no calls are needed), yet Definition 55 gives `N^*_S = ∞`. This is not a counterexample inside Theorem 56's stated subcase.
