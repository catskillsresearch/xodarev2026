**Theorem** (`theorem_C`). Let `PFS` be a `PriceFiltrationSignatureSpace` over a complete inner product space, and let `𝒦₁`, `𝒦₂` be subsets of `ℝ` whose closures contain the positive half-line `Ioi 0`. Then three facts hold: (1) for every `N`, price-filtration completeness on strike set `𝒦₁` at level `N` is equivalent to `PFS.completenessDepth ≤ (N : ℕ∞)`; (2) if `M : ℕ∞` is strictly below `completenessDepth`, completeness at level `M` on `𝒦₁` fails; (3) for every `N`, completeness on `𝒦₁` agrees with completeness on `𝒦₂` at the same level.

Proof. Apply `PFS.davis_obloj_bridge` to each strike set, obtaining for `𝒦₁` a density identity `hbridge₁` and the top-space identification `hVtop₁ : PFS.V_infty = ⊤`, and for `𝒦₂` at least the bridge `hbridge₂`. The core equivalence `hchar` is proved for `𝒦₁` after unfolding `IsCompleteOnPriceFiltration`, `augmentedMarketSpan`, and `completenessDepth`, then rewriting with `hbridge₁ N`.

For the forward implication, completeness at `N` means the augmented market span meets a topological-density condition; by definition of `completenessDepth` as an infimum over levels whose truncated signature span already closes to `V_infty`, the extended natural `N` is a lower bound for that infimum, hence `completenessDepth ≤ N` via `csInf_le` with the witnessing pair `⟨N, rfl, hN⟩`.

For the backward implication, assume `completenessDepth ≤ (N : ℕ∞)`. Let `S_good` be the set of extended levels `K` for which some finite `n` with `K = n` satisfies `(PFS.V_subspace n).topologicalClosure = PFS.V_infty`. If `S_good` were empty, then `sInf S_good` would be `⊤`, contradicting `not_top_le_coe` with `hle`. Thus `csInf_mem` yields some `n₀` realizing the infimum with closure identity `hn₀_cl` and coercion equation `hn₀_eq`, and `n₀ ≤ N` follows from comparing extended naturals. To show completeness at `N`, after the bridge rewrite one needs equality of topological closures `cl(V_subspace N) = cl(V_infty)`. The upper bound `cl(V_subspace N) ≤ V_infty = ⊤` is `le_top`. For the reverse, `V_infty ≤ cl(V_subspace N)` uses `hn₀_cl` backwards and monotonicity of topological closure along `V_subspace.mono PFS hn₀_le`.

The strict-depth statement packages as `fun M hlt hcomp ↦ not_le_of_gt hlt ((hchar M).mp hcomp)`: assuming completeness at `M` forces depth `≤ M`, contradicting strict inequality.

Strike-set independence is the final clause: unfold the same completeness and augmented-span predicates and rewrite with `hbridge₁ N` and `hbridge₂ N`, which express both completeness predicates by the same density of `V_subspace N` inside `V_infty`, so the two strike sets are equivalent at each `N`.
