# §2 Theorem A — existence, uniqueness, positivity

Paper: Theorem A / §3 (global strong solution of the signature SDE, Doléans–Dade representation, $L^2$ control under H1–H3).

Under H1–H3 the SDE $dS_t = S_t\langle\ell,\widehat W_t\rangle\,dB_t$, $S_0=s_0>0$, admits a unique global strong solution on $[0,T]$, strictly positive, with square-integrable supremum. The packaged solution is the pointwise Doléans–Dade exponential of the signature volatility. In the unwrapped signature-price predicate, almost every path is continuous on $[0,T]$, and at each time the process agrees with that exponential almost surely.
