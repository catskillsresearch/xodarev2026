**Definition** (`blackScholesModel`). We define the *Black–Scholes signature volatility model* with horizon $T > 0$, initial price $s_0 > 0$, and volatility parameter $\sigma_0 \neq 0$ to be the one-dimensional `SignatureVolatilityModel` with constant weight $w \equiv 1$, initial price $s_0$, and volatility series $\ell = \sigma_0\, e_{\emptyset}$ (coefficient only on the empty word), together with hypotheses H1–H3 verified from finite support and the nondegeneracy $\sigma_0 \neq 0$.
