**Theorem** (`section_7_1_black_scholes_completeness`). Let `H_S` be a complete real inner product space and `PFS : PriceFiltrationSignatureSpace H_S 1` (Black–Scholes depth `d = 1`). If the limiting static span is everything (`PFS.V_infty = ⊤`) and every basis observable `PFS.Y I` for `I : Word 2` already lies in `PFS.V_subspace 0`, then the completeness depth is zero: `PFS.completenessDepth = 0`.

Proof. Prove `PFS.completenessDepth ≤ 0` and combine with `zero_le`. Unfold `completenessDepth` as the infimum over levels `N` whose subspace closure equals `V_infty`. It suffices to show `0` belongs to that index set and satisfies the closure identity. For the upper bound `PFS.V_subspace 0 ≤ PFS.V_infty`, rewrite `V_infty` as `⊤` and use `le_top`. For the reverse inequality, show every element of `V_infty` lies in the topological closure of `V_subspace 0`. Unfold the definition of `V_infty` as a span; any generator is some `PFS.Y I` for a word `I`. By hypothesis `hBS_span`, each such generator is already in `V_subspace 0`, so the span is contained in `V_subspace 0`, and monotonicity of topological closure gives `V_infty ⊆ topologicalClosure (V_subspace 0)`. Together with `le_top` for the forward comparison, `le_antisymm` forces equality of `V_subspace 0` and `V_infty` in the closure sense at level `0`. Hence `csInf_le` pins the infimum to at most `0`.
