**Definition** (`infiniteGeometricSignatureModel`). We define an *infinite geometric signature volatility model* as a `SignatureVolatilityModel` with the following data: signature depth $d > 0$, horizon $T > 0$, initial price $s_0 > 0$, geometric weight sequence $w_n = r^n$ for some $r \ge 1$, a prescribed nonzero volatility series $\ell$, and constants $q \ge 0$, $C \ge 0$ with $q < r$ such that each level norm satisfies $\|\ell\|_n \le C\, q^n$. Hypothesis H1 is verified by summability of the weighted level-squares majorant $C^2 (q/r)^{2n}$, H2 from admissibility of the geometric weight, and H3 from $\ell \neq 0$.
