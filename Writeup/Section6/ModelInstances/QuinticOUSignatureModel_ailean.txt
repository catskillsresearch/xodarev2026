**Definition** (`quinticOUSignatureModel`). We define a *quintic OU signature volatility model* with horizon $T > 0$, initial price $s_0 > 0$, and quintic volatility series $\ell_{\mathrm{quintic}}$ on the two-letter alphabet to be the one-dimensional `SignatureVolatilityModel` with constant weight $w \equiv 1$, volatility $\ell = \ell_{\mathrm{quintic}}$ supported on words of length at most $5$, H1 from that finite support, H2 for the constant weight, and H3 from $\ell_{\mathrm{quintic}} \neq 0$.
